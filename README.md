# SPATIAL BLACKOUT
**Find the imposter before you run out of time!**
## Choosen themes
- Running Out of Time
- Dark / Light
## Instructions
There is a group of aliens on-screen. Among all of them, there is one who is different from the rest.\
Your mission is to find the alien and click it, but beware! There's a limited period of time to do it, and the more points that you get, the difficultier that it will get to find the alien.\
From time to time, there will be a blackout. That means that you won't be able to see anything for a few seconds.\
